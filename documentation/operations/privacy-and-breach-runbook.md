# Privacy rights and breach response

Privacy requests support export, correction, deletion, and consent withdrawal. Identity must be reverified before processing. Exports must use a private, expiring object; deletion must respect statutory financial/audit retention and anonymise rather than corrupt immutable ledgers.

For a suspected breach: contain access, preserve audit evidence, rotate affected secrets, identify data categories and subjects, assess notification obligations with counsel, notify through approved channels, document decisions, remediate the root cause, and complete a post-incident review. Never place affected PII in tickets, logs, analytics, or chat channels.
